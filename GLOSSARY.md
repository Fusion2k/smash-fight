# Smash Fight

A local multiplayer platform fighting game with original characters and art.

## Language

**Character**:
A distinct playable combatant with its own attributes, moves, and visual identity.

**Player**:
A person controlling a character during a match.

**Player slot**:
A place for one player and their chosen character in a local match, distinct from the controller assigned to it.

**Match**:
A contest between players in an arena, decided by the stock-based win condition.

**Arena**:
The space in which a match takes place, including its fighting surfaces and ring-out limits.

**Stock**:
One of a character's lives during a match, lost when the character leaves the arena's ring-out limits.

**Damage**:
The accumulated percentage that increases a character's vulnerability to knockback.

**Knockback**:
The launch caused by a hit, whose strength increases with the struck character's damage.

**Attack**:
A character's offensive action, performed on the ground or in the air.

**Air jump**:
A jump performed while a character is already airborne.

**Fast-fall**:
A deliberate increase in a character's falling speed.

**Recovery special**:
An attack that helps an airborne character return to the arena's fighting surfaces.

**Startup**:
The opening phase of an attack before it can hit an opponent.

**Active window**:
The phase of an attack during which it can hit an opponent.

**Attack recovery**:
The ending phase of an attack before the character can act again.

**Hitstun**:
The period after a hit during which the struck character cannot act.

**Hit pause**:
A brief freeze of the attacking and struck characters that emphasizes a hit.
_Avoid_: Hitstop

**Landing delay**:
The period after landing from an aerial attack before the character can act again.

**Ring-out**:
A character leaving the arena's ring-out limits, causing the loss of a stock.

**Input buffer**:
A short-lived record of a player's action press that allows the action to begin when the character becomes able to perform it.

**Ground-jump grace period**:
A brief period after leaving an edge during which a character can still perform a ground jump.

**One-way platform**:
A fighting surface a character can land on from above and pass through from below.

**Drop-through**:
A deliberate descent through the one-way platform supporting a character.
